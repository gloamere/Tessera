# Onboarding decision evidence

- Current completion baseline: 42% of started onboarding sessions.
- Support logs most often mention uncertainty about why company size and phone number are required.
- Sales needs company size before lead assignment; the phone number is not used until a customer requests a call.
- Removing all qualification fields would require a manual follow-up step for Sales.
- The team can expose a staged flow to 10% of new workspaces, but no experiment has run yet.
- Open question: whether a shorter first screen improves activation without reducing qualified-lead routing.
